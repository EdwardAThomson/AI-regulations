# Abu Dhabi Law No. 3 of 2024: Establishing the Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Technology Council (AIATC)

*Source note. The full Arabic legal text is the authoritative instrument; an accessible English
copy is not freely published. This note is compiled from official UAE government publications
(the Abu Dhabi Media Office) and reputable legal trackers. Verify against the original law.*

**Instrument:** Law No. 3 of 2024 (Emirate of Abu Dhabi)
**Issued:** 22 January 2024
**Issued by:** Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, in his capacity as Ruler of Abu Dhabi
**Effect:** Establishes the Artificial Intelligence and Advanced Technology Council (AIATC) as an
Abu Dhabi government body with legal personality and financial and administrative independence
**Status:** Binding emirate-level law (establishment/constitutive law), not an AI conduct
regulation

## Official sources

## What the law does

The law creates the AIATC and gives it a mandate to develop and implement policies, strategies,
research programmes, infrastructure and investments related to artificial intelligence and
advanced technology in the Emirate of Abu Dhabi. The Council is constituted with full legal,
financial and administrative independence to lead Abu Dhabi's AI and advanced-technology agenda.

## Stated responsibilities and objectives

- Set policies, strategies and regulations for AI and advanced technology in Abu Dhabi.
- Develop research programmes and establish research centres, in collaboration with local and
  global partners.
- Oversee and direct AI and advanced-technology projects, infrastructure and investments.
- Launch initiatives to attract leading companies, talent and investment to the sector, in
  support of economic growth and innovation.
- Enhance Abu Dhabi's position as a hub for AI and advanced technology.

## Notes

- This is a constitutive/institutional law: it establishes and empowers a body. It is not a
  conduct regulation; it sets no risk tiers, prohibited practices, obligations on developers or
  deployers, or penalties for AI use.
- Emirate-level (Abu Dhabi), distinct from the federal UAE AI Strategy and Charter, from the
  Dubai instruments, and from the ADGM free-zone regime that also sits within Abu Dhabi.
- The AIATC is the institutional anchor of Abu Dhabi's heavily investment-and-industrial-policy
  approach to AI (linked in practice to entities such as G42 and related vehicles).
