# Dubai Universal Blueprint for Artificial Intelligence (April 2024)

*Source note. No single primary document is published for the Blueprint; this note is compiled
from official UAE government publications (the u.ae government portal, the Dubai Media Office,
and the Dubai Executive Council) to support analysis. Verify against the original announcements.*

**Instrument:** Dubai Universal Blueprint for Artificial Intelligence
**Launched:** April 2024 (announced 29 April 2024)
**Launched by:** Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum, Crown Prince of Dubai and
Chairman of The Executive Council of Dubai
**Nature:** Annual (yearly) roadmap to accelerate AI adoption across Dubai government and economy
**Status:** Government strategy / programme; not legislation, not binding regulation

## Official sources

- UAE government portal: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/strategies-initiatives-and-awards/strategies-plans-and-visions/government-services-and-digital-transformation/dubai-universal-blueprint-for-ai
- Dubai Media Office / Protocol Office: https://www.protocol.dubai.ae/en/media-listing/news-events/hamdan-bin-mohammed-launches-dubai-universal-blueprint-for-artificial-intelligence/

## Purpose

Accelerate the adoption of AI applications across Dubai, enhance quality of life, and support the
Dubai Economic Agenda (D33). The Blueprint is positioned as a yearly plan: each year a new phase
of initiatives is announced, rather than a single fixed strategy document.

## Strategic objectives (four pillars)

1. Best application of AI uses in strategic sectors.
2. A global regulatory and governance sandbox for AI (positioning Dubai as a centre for AI
   governance and regulation).
3. Leadership in adopting AI in government work and services.
4. Creating the best environment for AI companies and talent.

## Phase One initiatives (2024)

1. Appoint a Chief AI Officer (CAIO) in every Dubai government entity. (22 CAIOs were appointed
   in June 2024.)
2. Establish AI and Web3 incubators.
3. Introduce an AI Week in educational institutions.
4. Introduce a dedicated Dubai AI commercial licence.
5. Allocate land for data centres.

## Economic context (D33 Agenda)

The Blueprint supports the Dubai Economic Agenda D33, which targets an annual AED 100 billion
contribution from digital transformation, positioning Dubai among the world's top three urban
economies and raising economic productivity by 50% through innovation and digital adoption.
Reporting at launch cited an aim to add roughly AED 100 billion / about USD 27 billion to the
economy by 2033 through AI.

## Notes

- Emirate-level (Dubai), issued by the Dubai Executive Council, distinct from the federal UAE AI
  Strategy 2031, the federal UAE Charter (2024), and from the DIFC, ADGM, and CBUAE regimes.
- Adoption-led and economic in framing; it is an implementation/acceleration programme rather
  than an ethics or regulatory instrument. The "regulatory and governance sandbox" pillar is its
  main governance-flavoured element.
